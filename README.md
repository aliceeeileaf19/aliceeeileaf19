## Featured projects

### [Transit Label Tuner](https://github.com/aliceeeileaf19/transit-label-tuner)

An offline-first visual editor that turns manual transit-diagram adjustments into reproducible move lists instead of one-off SVG edits.

- **Role:** Product design, interaction design, and front-end implementation
- **Focus:** Direct label manipulation, keyboard nudging, before/after comparison, and move-list export
- **Build:** Dependency-free HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and SVG with AI-assisted iteration
- [Try the live demo](https://aliceeeileaf19.github.io/transit-label-tuner/) · [Traditional Chinese documentation](https://github.com/aliceeeileaf19/transit-label-tuner/blob/main/README.zh-TW.md)

### [POPO Note](https://www.poponote.com/)

A content and community product in the Lang LIVE ecosystem, shaped from information structure and core flows through shipped interface.

- **Role:** Product and UI/UX design
- **Focus:** Information architecture, interaction flows, wireframes, and interface delivery

### [iQrator ecosystem](https://iqrator.org/zh/homepage/)

Product interfaces for an open-science organisation, connecting the homepage, Lab17, and open-source sites through one visual and interaction language.

- **Role:** Product and UI/UX design
- **Focus:** Cross-site information architecture, interaction patterns, and visual systems

### [Photo Gallop / 奔馬](https://github.com/aliceeeileaf19/photo-gallop)

An accessible Canvas interaction that turns 85 photographic fragments into a moving, browsable image archive.

- **Role:** Concept, product framing, interaction design, visual system, and front-end implementation
- **Core features:** Point-cloud gallop, proximity photo reveal, 85-image archive, keyboard and touch access, and reduced-motion support
- **Built with:** Canvas, dependency-free JavaScript, progressive image assets, and adaptive level of detail
- [Open the live demo](https://aliceeeileaf19.github.io/photo-gallop/) · [Read the source and asset licenses](https://github.com/aliceeeileaf19/photo-gallop#licensing)
